Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Spoons Compass is made by Christian Garry. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Location and compass

The app uses your phone's location and compass to find the nearest Wetherspoon pub and show its direction and distance. These calculations happen on your device. The app does not send your location or compass readings to me or anyone else, or save a history of them.

The app requests location access while you use it. It uses the location accuracy you allow in your phone's settings.

Saved preferences

The app saves two choices on your phone: light or dark mode, and metric or imperial distances. These settings do not contain your location or compass readings.

Accounts and tracking

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. It does not access your contacts, calendar, photos, camera or microphone. These privacy practices apply to all users, including children.

Offline pub data

The pub locations come from OpenStreetMap contributors and are included in the app. Finding a pub does not require an internet connection or send a request to OpenStreetMap.

Opening this website

The privacy policy link opens this website in your browser. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security under GitHub's privacy statement. The app does not include your location or compass readings in the link.

Location permission

You can change or revoke location access at any time in your phone's settings:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Spoons Compass → Permissions.
  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Spoons Compass.

Without location permission, the app cannot find the nearest pub and will ask you to enable it.

Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@christiangarry.com. If you contact me, I receive your email address and message and use them to respond to your request.

You can also report app problems on GitHub.

Unofficial app

Spoons Compass is unofficial and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or in any way connected to J D Wetherspoon plc.
