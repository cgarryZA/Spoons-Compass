All the pub locations are already on your phone. Open Spoons Compass and use your GPS and compass to find the nearest J D Wetherspoon, without mobile data or Wi-Fi. Explore a new town or take a wander somewhere familiar — you might find a Spoons you never knew was there.

Follow the compass

  1. Open the app and allow location access when asked.
  2. Hold your phone flat. The arrow points towards the nearest pub; its name and distance appear above the compass.
  3. Choose your own route. The arrow and distance update as you move.

The distance is measured in a straight line. Tap it to switch between metres and kilometres or yards and miles. Tap the background to change between light and dark mode. The app remembers both choices.

UK pub locations are included with the app, using data from OpenStreetMap. Your location and compass readings are processed on your phone and aren't saved or sent to a server.

Join the beta

Try Spoons Compass on Android before the public launch.

  1. Email support@christiangarry.com to join, and include the Google account you use on your Android phone.
  2. On that phone, tap Join the beta above, then Become a tester.
  3. Follow the link to Google Play and install Spoons Compass.
  4. Stay opted in for at least 14 days. Try the compass on a few walks and send any feedback to the support email above.

Pub coverage is limited to the United Kingdom, including Northern Ireland. Location access is required, and the arrow needs a working compass sensor. An iPhone version is in development.
